About Me

Hocine Oulmi is a visual artist, muralist, and graffiti artist from Algeria, born in 1995. He graduated from the School of Fine Arts in Algiers and has over 14 years of experience in the art field, currently working between the United Arab Emirates and Algeria.

His work focuses on large-scale murals, street graffiti, Arabic calligraphy, live painting, ceramic design, and mixed-media artwork.

He has collaborated with brands such as Porsche, Red Bull, Alpha X, and Vonto, alongside numerous commercial and private clients.
